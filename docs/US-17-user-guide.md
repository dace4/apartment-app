# US-17 — Schedule repair and maintenance: user guide

Property managers book repair and maintenance services for the apartments
they manage, and the app reminds them before each service.

## Who can book

- A **Property Manager** can book for the apartments they are assigned to.
- Homeowners, renters and buyers do not see **Repair & maintenance**
  (decided at the Sprint 3 review).

If you own or manage no apartment yet, the booking page says so.

## Book a service

1. Open **Services → Repair & maintenance** and select **Schedule service**.
2. Choose the apartment, the category (plumbing, electrical, heating,
   appliances or other) and describe the problem (5–500 characters).
3. Choose when to be reminded: **1 day before**, **1 hour before** and/or
   **5 minutes before**.
4. Choose a date. Services take place Monday to Friday, from tomorrow up to
   60 days ahead.
5. Scroll the **Time** wheel to a free 30-minute slot. The wheel only shows
   free times between 08:00–12:00 and 13:00–17:00; times you already booked
   are left out.
6. Select **Book service**.

## Reschedule or cancel

Open the service in **Repair & maintenance**. Until it starts, you can select
**Reschedule** (new date, time or reminders) or **Cancel service**.

## Reminders

When a reminder time comes:

- a banner appears at the top of every tab, and
- a **Maintenance** notification is added to **Profile → Notifications**
  (with a red dot on the Profile tab). Tap it to open the service.

If several reminders became due while the app was closed, only the one
closest to the service is shown. The notification always stays in your
inbox; switching **Maintenance** off in **Profile → Notifications** only
stops its red badge. Reminders are created while the app is running, at or after the
reminder time and before the service starts.
