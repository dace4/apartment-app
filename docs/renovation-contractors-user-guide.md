# Renovation contractors

Sign in with a verified email and a Homeowner role, then open **Services > Renovation contractors**.
Browse contractors across Geneva, Lausanne and Zurich, supplied by Firebase.
Choose a city to filter, or **All cities** to reset. Each entry shows its trade,
description and selectable email address.
Booking and messaging are not included.

An internet connection is required. If loading fails, resolve the connection or access issue and tap **Try again**.
An empty collection shows an empty-state message. The directory reads its data from Firebase.
