# US-14 — Property FAQ: user guide

The FAQ answers common questions about searching, visiting, applying for,
renting and buying apartments in HomeFlow.

## Open the FAQ

- From **Profile → FAQ**, or
- from any apartment: select **Questions? Read the FAQ** at the bottom of its
  page.

## Find an answer

1. Type a word in **Search the FAQ** (for example *deposit*, *visit* or
   *documents*). Questions and answers containing it are listed as you type.
   Select the **×** to clear the search.
2. Or select a topic: **Searching**, **Visits**, **Applying**, **Rent & costs**,
   **Owners & managers** or **Account**. Select it again, or **All**, to see
   every question.
3. Tap a question to show its answer. Many answers have a link (for example
   **My applications** or **Mortgage calculator**) that opens the page where
   you can act on it. Links only appear for pages your roles allow.

If nothing matches, the FAQ says so and offers **Show all questions**.

## A question about one apartment

When you open the FAQ from an apartment, a **Question about this apartment?**
card appears at the top. Select **Contact** to send its advertiser a message.
This card is shown to renters and buyers, who can contact advertisers.
