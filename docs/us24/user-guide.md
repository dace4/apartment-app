# US-24 — Publish an apartment

1. Sign in, verify your email, and select the Homeowner role in your profile.
2. Open Apartments and select **Add apartment** in the top bar.
3. Enter a title, address, city, rent/sale type, CHF price, number of rooms,
   surface in square metres and description. Add amenities if relevant.
4. Select **Add pictures** and choose JPEG or PNG files. Include at least three
   different pictures; at most six are allowed. Originals may be up to 10 MiB
   and 32 megapixels each. The app compresses them for publication.
5. Check the previews. The first picture is the cover. Use **Remove picture**
   to remove a selection; remove and re-add pictures if you want a different cover.
6. Select **Publish apartment**. Wait for the confirmation and apartment details.
   Swipe pictures or use Previous picture / Next picture to browse the gallery.

New listings are immediately available. Price means monthly rent for rental
listings and total sale price for sale listings. Older listings may show only
one picture. Pictures that cannot load show a home placeholder.

If validation fails, correct the message shown beside Publish. If selection fails,
select readable JPEG/PNG pictures again. If publication fails, the form and
pictures remain on screen: reconnect, confirm your verified Homeowner account,
and retry. Avoid closing the form before retrying; the draft is kept only while
that screen stays open. Retrying from the same form does not duplicate a listing.

If Add apartment is missing, verify your email and check the Homeowner role.
Renters, buyers and property managers without that role cannot publish.
