# US-06: your profile and roles

During registration, enter your full name and phone number. They are saved in your
profile and fill in the forms that ask for them: the name and phone of a new
application (Apply and Apply immediately) and the phone of an insurance agent request.
You can still change them in a form; that does not change your profile. Editing an
application keeps the name and phone written on that application.
In Profile, tap Contact details to open it, add or change them and press Save contact
details. Closed, the section shows your saved name and phone.
Accounts created before this change have no name or phone yet: their forms start
empty until they add them in Profile.

During registration, also select one or more roles: Buyer, Renter, Homeowner. The Admin role is
not offered: the project team gives it in the Firebase console (see
admin-maintenance-user-guide.md).
Open the confirmation email and verify it before using the app.
Existing accounts choose roles once after verification. Save roles to continue.
In Profile, tap Your roles to open it (closed, it lists your saved roles), change the
checkboxes and press Save roles. At least one role is required.
If saving fails, retry. If account creation succeeded but verification email or profile
saving failed, use the signed-in verification screen (Resend email) and role selection;
do not register a second account. Sign in if the app was closed.
A profile loading failure shows Retry and Log out. Selecting Homeowner never assigns a
property.
